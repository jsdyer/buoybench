What was expected?

Marine forecasts cover an area. A buoy measures one point within or near that area. Read them together to investigate conditions; this is not an automated forecast accuracy score.

Keep the timing honest.

Measured, issued, captured

Observation time is when the buoy measured conditions. Forecast issue time comes from ECCC. Capture time is when this project retrieved the record. Select an older capture to see the evidence available then.

Make the bench your own

The station watchlist and forecast-area mappings are editable in web/config.json. Add another buoy, run the refresh workflow and it appears here. Older retained stations remain available. Customization instructions.

Source and limits

Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada. Contains information licenced under the Data Server End-use Licence of ECCC. Observations are provisional. Missing or non-passing QA measurements appear as gaps. This research record is not a navigation or warning service; check current official marine forecasts and warnings before heading out.

Scheduled collection is requested every six hours. Captures can be delayed or missed; the displayed dates are authoritative. The page loads published files from CoolCode. Searches and imports stay in this tab; imported receipts are not uploaded or stored automatically.
